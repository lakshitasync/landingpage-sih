1. Vamana Karma (Therapeutic Emesis)

Primary indication: Kapha-predominant disorders, Psoriasis, Asthma.

Purva Karma: Snehapana with Mahatiktaka Ghrita (3 to 7 days) → Abhyanga & Swedana.

Pradhana Karma: Madanaphala Pipli Yoga administration with Yashtimadhu Phanta.

Paschat Karma: Samsarjana Krama dietary transition for 3–7 days.

🌿

2. Virechana Karma (Therapeutic Purgation)

Primary indication: Pitta-predominant disorders, Jaundice, Eczema.

Purva Karma: Deepana-Pachana → Snehapana (Tiktaka Ghrita) → Abhyanga.

Pradhana Karma: Trivrit Lehyam / Icchabhedi Rasa administration in morning.

Paschat Karma: Vega counting and Samsarjana diet schedule.

💧

3. Basti Karma (Medicated Enemas)

Primary indication: Vata disorders, Sciatica, Hemiplegia, Parkinsonism.

Niruha Basti: Erandmooladi / Dashamula decoction + Honey + Saindhava + Sneha.

Anuvasana Basti: Sahacharadi / Mahanarayana Taila (60ml retention).

Courses: Yoga Basti (8 days), Kala Basti (16 days), Karma Basti (30 days).

🌸

4. Nasya & 5. Raktamokshana

Primary indication: Urdhvajatrugata (ENT/Neuro) & Blood disorders (Rakta Dushti).

Nasya: Anu Taila / Shadbindu Taila instillation into nostrils with facial swedana.

Raktamokshana: Sterile Jalaukavacharana (Medicinal Leech) and Siravyadha venepuncture.

Peer-Reviewed Classical Case

Management of Sciatica (Gridhrasi)

Patient Profile: 54-Year-Old Male, Chronic radiating lumbar pain along sciatic nerve trajectory.

Nidana & Dosha Dushti: Vata-Kapha Pradhana, Asthi-Majja Dhatu Dushti.

Asthavidha Pariksha: Nadi: Vata-Kapha, Jihwa: Saama (Mild coated), Mala: Vibandha.

Clinical Regimen Administered:
  • Siravyadha: 40ml bloodletting at 4 Angula above ankle joint on affected limb.
  • Basti: Erandmooladi Niruha Basti alternating with Sahacharadi Anuvasana for 8 days.
  • Samana Aushadhi: Yogaraj Guggulu (2 BD) + Rasnasaptak Kwatha (30ml BD).

Outcome & Follow-up (Day 14):

Straight Leg Raise (SLR) improved from 30° to 75°. Pain scale reduced from 8/10 to 1/10.
